# Handrail imagined screens

Five imagined screens for the proposal "Handrail: a floor and a path". None of these exist. They are described precisely enough to be drawn as static mock-ups. All example data belongs to one fictional household, the Ekblad family, with no relation to any real person. Every household count and registry row is illustrative, year three, and is labelled so.

**The fictional household.** Guardians: Sara and Jonas. Children: Ines (7, age band under 13, milestone Choosing), Teo (11, age band under 13, milestone Meeting), Maja (14, age band 13 to 15, milestone Noticing, so her parents see totals only). Devices: a shared tablet (Ines), a Switch and the family television (Teo, shared), a phone and a school laptop (Maja), a PlayStation in the living room (shared, not participating). Subscribed profile: "Swedish health guidance" with local changes. Evening reference date for every screen: Sunday 18 October 2026.

Rules for every screen. No child's face. No map. No message text. No search term. No third child's name. No real brand logos; device and app names appear as plain text. Every number a parent sees is one the child can also see, and every enforcement state is shown to both in the same words. Grey means "not participating"; amber is reserved for a certified platform's own unknown state. UK English, 24-hour clock.

## V1. A parent's weekly view (Sara's phone, the controller app)

**Purpose.** Show what kind of time it was, not only how much, and show honestly whether each rule landed. Everything on this screen lives in the home.

**Layout, top to bottom.**

1. **Header.** "Ekblad household" left, a settings cog right. Beneath, three child tabs: "Ines 7", "Teo 11", "Maja 14". "Teo 11" is selected. Under the selected tab, two chips: "under 13" and "Meeting". The "Maja 14" tab, if tapped, shows only a total against budget and the enforcement strip, with the line "Maja reached Noticing in April. Per-app detail is hers."

2. **Week chart.** Seven vertical bars, Mon 12 to Sun 18 October. Each bar is stacked in three shades with a legend above: "full rate", "half rate (creative, educational)", "double rate (endless feed, autoplay)". A thin horizontal line across all bars labelled "allowance 120 min (school days 90)". Example heights: Mon 85 of which 40 half-rate; Tue 92 of which 30 half-rate; Wed 60 with a hatched band 17:00 to 18:00 labelled on tap "Locked, dinner guests, Jonas"; Thu 88; Fri 110 of which 20 half-rate; Sat 124 with a small "+20" marker above the line labelled on tap "Grant 20, finished homework early, Sara"; Sun 70 so far. No double-rate time appears for Teo, and the legend notes "none this week".

3. **Today's ledger, plain lines.** Header "Sunday 18 October".
   - "08:00 Allowance 120 min (weekend)"
   - "10:12 to 10:58 Minecraft, Switch, 46 min, half rate (creative), counted 23"
   - "13:30 to 14:17 Television, 47 min, full rate (device total; the television reports no apps)"
   - "16:05 Request: Rocket Racers, play, friends-only. Pending"
   - "Remaining 50 min"

4. **Guide card, muted background.** Heading "A suggestion". Body: "Teo has stopped at his limit without a lock on 24 of the last 30 days. Families on your profile usually add 15 weekday minutes at this point." Three buttons: "Add 15", "Edit", "Not now". Small line beneath: "Worked out on this phone from the last 30 days of the ledger and your profile. Not stored. Nothing about Teo left this house."

5. **Request card.** "Teo asked at 16:05 to play Rocket Racers." Three tag chips with counts: "friends-only multiplayer (61)", "chat-with-strangers possible (14)", "voice chat (22)", with a small label "votes illustrative, year three". A four-line draft headed "If you want to talk first": "Who can hear you in voice chat. What to do if someone asks where you live. How to leave a server. Tell us if it feels wrong." Buttons: "Yes, friends-only, voice off", "Talk first", "Ask him why".

6. **Enforcement health strip.** Four device rows, each with a dot and a line: "Switch: confirmed 16:10" (green); "Television: confirmed 16:10" (green); "Tablet: not Teo's" (grey); "PlayStation: not participating, network control only" (grey). A tap target "What do the colours mean?" opens the rule: "Green: confirmed by read-back. Amber: a certified platform has not confirmed; shown as locked, never as free. Grey: not a Handrail platform; the controller can only cut its network."

7. **Footer.** "Raw ledger kept 31 days, then deleted. Milestones kept as a name and a date only. Nothing leaves this house without your say. Teo can see everything on this screen."

## V2. A child's path (Teo's tablet, the controller app, age 11)

**Purpose.** The child sees the same facts as the parent, in his own words, and can ask or disagree. This is the household's own app, not a platform surface. On a certified platform Teo would see only the fixed notice: "A household plan applies. Set by your guardian. Open your Handrail app for the reason."

**Layout.**

1. **Header.** "Teo's road". Right: a sun icon three-quarters full with the text "50 minutes left today".

2. **The road.** A winding path across a simple landscape with six stepping stones. Stones 1 and 2 are lit and dated: "Stopping, Mum and Dad agreed March 2025", "Choosing, agreed June 2026". Stone 3 glows: "Meeting. Next: a talk with Mum or Dad about strangers in games." Beneath stone 3, two lines worked out from the last month of the ledger and marked "this month, not saved": "Stopped on time, school days this month: 14 of 20", "Played friends-only: 3 weeks". Stones 4 to 6 are outlined and labelled "Noticing", "Judging", "Owning" with no dates. Small type: "When you reach Noticing, which apps you use becomes yours alone to see."

3. **What you can open now.** A row of plain tiles: "Minecraft (counts half)", "YouTube Kids", "Drawing". A second row greyed: "Rocket Racers: asked at 16:05, waiting" with a hand icon; "Fortnite: ask first" with a hand icon; "Television after 20:00: bedtime" with a moon icon.

4. **Why? button on each greyed tile.** Tapping "Fortnite" shows the ledger line in child language: "Mum and Dad set this to 'ask first' in June because other families say it has chat with strangers. You can ask any time." Beneath it a button "I don't agree", which adds the line "Teo disagreed with the Fortnite rule, 18 Oct 16:40" to the household ledger that both can see. This stays inside the controller and never touches a platform.

5. **Ask for more time.** A button, and beneath it a short history: "Sat 17 Oct: asked 20 min, Mum said yes (homework done)". "Wed 14 Oct: asked 30 min, Dad said not today (dinner guests)".

6. **Enforcement health strip, the same lines as Sara sees.** "Switch: confirmed 16:10" (green); "Television: confirmed 16:10" (green); "PlayStation: not participating, network control only" (grey). If a device were amber it would read here exactly as on V1: "Rule in force: locked. Tablet has not confirmed since 14:02."

7. **Footer line.** "Everything your parents see about screens, you can see here. Nobody reads your messages. Nobody sees where you are. Nothing you tell a grown-up is written down here."

## V3. A regulator's certification dashboard (Ofcom analyst, desktop)

**Purpose.** Show that the mark is inspectable, that enforcement has a process, and that the dashboard cannot see any child or household.

**Layout, three panels with a banner.**

**Banner across the top.** "Handrail certified implementations. This registry lists platforms, controllers, guides and default profiles, not households. No household, child or account is identifiable here or anywhere in the steward. The steward publishes no household data."

**Left panel: registry table.** Columns: Entry, Level, Certified, Suite version, Last field report, Status. Example rows (illustrative, 2030 dates):
- "Example TV OS | 2 | 2030-03-03 | 1.x | 2030-09 | Listed"
- "Social service A, teen accounts | 2 | 2030-06-20 | 1.x | 2030-10 | Notified (unknown-as-unlocked 1.4% over 30 days), curing until 2031-01"
- "Home router firmware B | 1 | 2029-02-14 | 1.x | 2030-08 | Listed"
- "Reference household controller | controller | 2028-11-02 | 1.x | n/a | Listed"
- "Default profile, UK | version 2 | published by Ofcom 2028-04 | n/a | n/a | Current"
Filters above the table: "Jurisdiction: UK", "Type: all", "Status: all". A legend for Status: "Listed, Notified, Curing, Delisted (prospective only)".

**Centre panel: two charts.** Upper: a bar chart "Certified implementations by level, registry data" with bars for Level 1 to Level 4 and a twelve-month trend line, example values 3, 2, 0, 0, labelled "illustrative, 2030". Lower: a world map shaded in three tones with the legend "mark cited in guidance", "under consultation", "no citation yet", and a fourth hatched tone "path requires primary legislation"; UK in the first tone, EU in the second, Australia hatched, others in the third.

**Right panel: field report.** Heading "The one rule the mark enforces". A table of counts only, no identifiers, no timestamps: Platform, "writes", "reported unknown", "later found unlocked while a lock was in force", Ratio. Example: "Example TV OS | 1,480,000 | 1,204 | 0 | 0.0%"; "Social service A | 620,000 | 2,900 | 8,700 | 1.4% (notified)". Beneath, "Synthetic household audit": "scripted test accounts run against each listed platform; last run 2030-10-02; traces held by the steward; no real household involved". A line: "Threshold: 1% of locks over 30 days, certified controllers only. Process: notice, 90-day cure, independent appeal."

**Footer.** "Annual re-test due dates" as a small calendar strip. A link "Download the registry as open data".

## V4. A platform's compliance view (product manager's console, a games platform)

**Purpose.** Show that Level 2 is a bounded engineering task and that the platform receives nothing it should not.

**Layout.**

1. **Header.** "Handrail conformance: Level 2, certified 3 March 2030 against suite 1.x, re-test due March 2031 against the same major version." A single switch labelled "Accept Handrail household plans" set to on. Beside it a checklist headed "What Level 3 needs": "grants (not started)", "child requests (in progress)", "per-app rules (not started)".

2. **Field mapping table.** Columns: Inbound plan field, How we honour it, Reported back as. Rows:
   - "Daily allowance (15-minute steps) | native | applied"
   - "Allowed windows (30-minute slots) | native | applied"
   - "Bedtime | native | applied"
   - "Age band (under 13, 13 to 15, 16 to 17, 18+) | sets content ceiling | applied"
   - "Guardian's country | selects which default profile applies, with our own geo signal | applied"
   - "Per-app block, by app id | mapped to title allow list | applied"
   - "Count half / count double | not in our declared capabilities, so not sent | n/a"
   - "Statutory default profile (no plan present) | native, from the registry, versioned by jurisdiction | applied"
   A note: "We never receive tags. The household controller resolves them."

3. **Read-back log, last 24 hours.** Columns: Received, Object, Applied, Confirmed, Status. Example lines: "16:10:02 | override lock, pairwise id 8f2… | 16:10:03 | 16:10:04 | confirmed"; "16:10:02 | override lock, pairwise id 3a9… | 16:10:03 | timed out | unknown (reported)". A summary bar: "4,000 writes, 3,997 confirmed, 3 unknown (0.08%), 0 silent failures". An amber note on the 3: "Read-back timed out beyond declared window; traces kept 12 months, pseudonymous, no content." Small type: "Pairwise ids are unique to this platform and cannot be matched with any other service."

4. **Capabilities declared, public.** "lock now: yes", "daily budget: yes", "windows: yes (30-minute slots)", "per-app usage: no", "child requests: no (Level 3 in progress)".

5. **Box headed "What we never receive".** "Name. Date of birth. Location beyond country. Identity documents. Message content. Household identifier. Competence or milestones. Reasons or who decided. Only a pairwise child id, an age band, a country and the effects of a plan." One line for legal, in quotation marks: "This account carries a plan from a steward-credentialled controller. Applying it discharges the minimum-age duty for this account; our other duties are unchanged."

6. **Box headed "What this claim supports".** Three rows: "UK Online Safety Act: supports, where the s.214A regulations name the route"; "DSA Article 28 guidelines: supports"; "Australia Social Media Minimum Age Act: does not address; the Act has no parental route and the path would need an amendment". Each with a plain tick or the words "does not address".

7. **Footer banner.** "Data received through Handrail may be used only to apply the plan, read back, log conformance, prevent abuse and run child-safety features. Never for advertising, ad measurement, cross-service profiling, sale or sharing. Field audit is by the steward's synthetic test accounts; no household log leaves this platform." Button: "Run conformance suite". Button: "Submit to steward".

## V5. The guide's first conversation (Jonas's phone, set-up day)

**Purpose.** Show that set-up is three questions, that no date of birth is ever asked, that devices join by choice and not by discovery, and that the floor applies before anyone does anything.

**Layout.** A chat screen headed "Setting up Handrail for your house". The guide's messages on the left, Jonas's choices as large buttons.

1. **Guide.** "Before we start: every child account on a certified platform already has the statutory default profile your regulator published. Nothing you do here can give a child less than that. What you can do is give them a path above it. I only need three things."

2. **Guide.** "Which age band is each child in?" Three rows, each with four band buttons "under 13", "13 to 15", "16 to 17", "18 plus" and a name field. Jonas has entered "Ines" with "under 13" selected, "Teo" with "under 13", "Maja" with "13 to 15". No date picker anywhere on the screen. Small type: "Pre-filled from the family accounts your devices already hold. I never see a birthday." Beneath, a country field showing "Sweden" with the note "used only to pick the right floor".

3. **Guide.** "Which of these sounds most like your house?" Three profile cards, each with a small "households: illustrative, year three" label:
   - "Swedish health guidance (890 households): two hours a day at 6 to 12, three at 13 to 18, bedtime by age, no chat with strangers under 13."
   - "Strict under 10 (2,140 households): no chat, no purchases, one hour on school days."
   - "Educational first (1,320 households): learning and creative apps count half, bedtime 21:00."
   The first card is selected. A small line: "You can change any rule for any child afterwards. Your changes always beat the profile."

4. **Guide.** "Which devices should join? I have not looked at your network. Add each one yourself, and the child will see it listed." A list Jonas builds with an "Add a device" button, each entry with an honest enforcement note:
   - "Shared tablet: native controls, confirmed by read-back" (added)
   - "Switch: native controls, set in the console's own app; I will ask you to confirm" (added)
   - "Television: smart plug and allow-listed network control, confirmed" (added)
   - "PlayStation: not participating; network control only, shown grey" (added)
   - "Maja's phone: OS age band already 13 to 15; Maja must accept the plan on her phone, and from Noticing you will see totals only" (waiting for Maja)
   - "School laptop: managed by the school. Not eligible." (cannot be added, greyed)

5. **Guide, closing message.** "That's enough to start. Here is what I will never see: what they watch, what they say, where they are, their birthdays. I work on this phone; if you ever turn on the remote helper I get only budgets, tag lookups and milestone dates, and keep nothing. Here is what they will always see: everything I show you. Bedtime is the rule I will protect first and hand over last. You can delete all of this in one tap, and when a child leaves the plan they choose to export or delete their milestones, and everything else goes."

6. **Footer.** Two buttons: "Start" and "Show me the ledger Maja will see". Beneath, in small type: "Handrail is an open standard. This app is one of several certified to run it."
