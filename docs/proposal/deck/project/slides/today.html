This is where parents are now. Five islands. Each company gives you a timer, a bedtime and a lock, for its own devices only. Family Link counts per device, so two hours means two hours on the tablet and two more on the phone. Apple’s Screen Time speaks to nothing outside Apple. Microsoft cannot see the Switch. Nobody can see the PlayStation. Of eleven tools we checked, nine can set a daily limit and not one can see another vendor’s device or let a parent join them. The child in the middle is untouched.
