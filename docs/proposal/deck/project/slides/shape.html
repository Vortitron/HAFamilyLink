What Handrail is

Handrail is a plan and a language, not an app

Handrail provides

The plan. One written format for a child’s rules, each with its reason.

The language. How a device or service receives a plan and reports back what it did.

The mark. A public test suite and a registry of what passed, run by a steward.

The guides. A Swedish default plan, community profiles, and an optional AI guide.

Who shows it to the parent

The app the family already uses, once certified. Screen Time, Family Link or a console app can then set the plan for every screen in the house.

Or an independent app, website or home hub that connects to all of them.

The family chooses, and can change its mind without losing the plan.

Who follows it

Every certified device, console, app and platform. Each applies the plan on its own screens and reports back.

A vendor may not insist that a parent uses its app.

A vendor may not give another company’s app fewer controls than its own.

Handrail itself puts no app in front of parents. It publishes the standard, the test suite and free reference software that any vendor can build on.

A word on what Handrail is, because it is easy to picture it as one more app. It is not. It is three things. A written plan: one child’s rules, each with a reason.
