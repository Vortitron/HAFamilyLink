Australia is the only country with a ban long enough to measure. Three months in, use fell by about five points. And the holes are not only clever teenagers. About 11 per cent said a parent helped them stay on; 7.4 per cent used a VPN. In the UK, where a child uses a VPN, 43 per cent have a parent paying for it. Parents do this because their child’s friends are all there. So the ban makes many parents part of the workaround, and each new check brings a new one.
