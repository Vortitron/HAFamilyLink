The vision · over time

The plan grows with the child, from a first tablet to a first phone

Age 2

A tablet with three apps and a timer. Nothing else opens.

The parent chooses everything.

Age 6

Reading and drawing apps count half. Videos stop at 30 minutes.

The child sees the timer and the reason.

Age 10

Friends-only games. Chat with strangers blocked. Homework apps first on school days.

The child can ask for more, and sees the answer.

Age 14

A phone. A budget the child helped set. Parents see totals only.

The child sets her own bedtime and keeps it.

The same plan, loosened step by step by the parent as the child shows they can stop, choose and judge. At sixteen there is nothing left to switch off. Example family; every rule is the parent’s to change.

Handrail, draft for discussion, October 2026 · Proposal sections 3.2 and 3.3

3 / 20

This is not a tool for teenagers and social media. It starts with a two year old and a tablet that opens three apps and nothing else. At six the drawing app counts half and videos stop after half an hour. At ten, friends-only games, chat with strangers blocked, homework apps first. At fourteen, a phone, a budget she helped set, and her parents see totals only. The rules loosen as the child shows she can stop, choose and judge. By sixteen there is nothing left to switch off, and nothing was ever switched on behind her back.
