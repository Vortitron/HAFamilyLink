The proposal · who switches it on

A parent switches it on, and can switch it off

Chosen by a parent

Switched on in whichever certified app the family uses, under the family account they already have. It covers the children on that account and nobody else.

Switched off the same way

The parent who set it up turns it off in the same place. An adult with no plan is simply an adult.

Where the law sets an age

Certified services apply it to child accounts by default. Handrail complies with a limit. It does not need one to be useful.

Handrail was built for a two year old with a tablet, long before any ban was discussed.

Who turns this on? A parent or guardian, in whichever certified app the family uses, under the family account they already have. It covers only the children on that account. The same parent can turn it off in the same place. An adult with no plan is just an adult. Where a law does set an age, certified services apply it to child accounts by default, so Handrail complies with a limit. It does not need one. It was designed for a two year old with a tablet, long before any ban.
