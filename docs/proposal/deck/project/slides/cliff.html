This is the cost no survey measures. A ban is a wall with a cliff behind it. Children route around it, and when the birthday comes nothing has been learned. The UN children’s rights committee asks states to respect children’s evolving capacities and to favour empathy over prohibition or control. The research base is no better: of 40 restriction experiments reviewed by Lind, Schueller and Odgers, none had a mean participant age under 18. We are legislating for children with no evidence gathered from children.
