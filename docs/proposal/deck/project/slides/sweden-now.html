The missing tool: a way to apply the guidance across every screen.

Regeringen 2 Sept 2024 · SOU 2026:35 · Skolverket June 2026 · Internetstiftelsen, Barnen och internet 2025 · Commission KIDS Act FAQ

8 / 19

The Public Health Agency gave families clear hour limits by age in 2024. The inquiry proposed in June that no child holds a social media account before the year they turn 15, from 2028, with no punishment for children or parents. Since August, compulsory schools collect phones for the whole school day. What is missing is the tool. Nine in ten parents know the recommended hours, more than eight in ten worry they cannot protect their child, and one in five do not know how.
