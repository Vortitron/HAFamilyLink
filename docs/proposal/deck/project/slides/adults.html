A minimum age makes every adult prove they are not a child. Ofcom counted over 69 million age checks on 32 UK services in six months. France’s constitutional court struck down its law in August, mainly as a disproportionate limit on expression, and because it forced every user to prove their age with no legal conditions or limits. That is the legal risk you carry too. And the data leaks: about 70,000 government ID photos, by Discord’s own count, from a vendor handling age-verification appeals.
