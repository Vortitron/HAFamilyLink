A minimum age makes every adult prove they are not a child. Ofcom counted over 69 million age checks on 32 UK services in six months. France’s constitutional court struck down its law in August, mainly as a disproportionate limit on expression, and because it forced every user to prove their age with no legal conditions or limits. A UK scheme that checks every user invites the same privacy challenge. The checks also leak data: about 70,000 government ID photos, by Discord’s own count, from a vendor handling age-verification appeals.
