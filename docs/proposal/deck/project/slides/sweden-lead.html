Here is why Sweden can lead rather than follow. Sweden has already shown that guidance plus parents moves behaviour: children aged 9 to 12 use screens forty minutes less a day than in 2022, and the Public Health Agency credits the recommendations. The Commission’s KIDS Act proposes a parent-controlled account for 13 and 14 year olds with a time limit and approved contacts. That is a household plan. Nobody has said how it works across vendors. Sweden can show that, with evidence, before the rules are fixed. The EU may still push a ban. Then we argue from results.
