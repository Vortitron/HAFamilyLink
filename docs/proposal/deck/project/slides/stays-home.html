This matters to the Chancellor of Justice and to the Data Protection Authority. A platform never receives a name, a date of birth, a document or a location. It receives the plan: an age band, the country, the budget and the rules, under a code that is different for every platform, so two companies can never match the same child. The full record stays in the home and is deleted after a month unless the family chooses otherwise. Compare that with checking every adult’s identity at the door of every service.
