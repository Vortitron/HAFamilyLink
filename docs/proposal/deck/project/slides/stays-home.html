This matters to the Chancellor of Justice and to IMY, the Authority for Privacy Protection. A platform never receives a name, a date of birth, a document or a location. It receives the plan: an age band, the country, the budget and the rules, under a code that is different for every platform, so two companies can never match the same child. The full record stays with whichever controller the family chose, a vendor’s app or a box at home, held under the family’s own account. The family can move it to another controller at any time, and it is deleted after a month unless they choose otherwise.
