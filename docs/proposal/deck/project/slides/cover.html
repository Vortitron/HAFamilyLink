Handrail

Keep the floor. Add a path.

Hold on while learning. Let go when ready.

An open standard, with certification, for guiding children online. It sits beside a minimum age, not against it.

Fronting the paper “Handrail: a floor and a path”, draft 0.3, 21 October 2026

Thank you for ten minutes. You are about to legislate a minimum age. Nothing in this deck asks you to drop it. It asks you to add a second route beside it. The name is the idea: a handrail is what a child holds while learning the stairs and lets go of when ready. Three slides on what a ban costs, seven on what we propose, then a calendar, three asks and a price. The paper is the backing document.
