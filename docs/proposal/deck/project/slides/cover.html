Parents in control. One plan covers all their child’s screens.

Handrail guides a child’s screens from a first tablet at 2 to independence. It is an open standard: shared rules any company can follow. Devices and apps that pass its tests are certified and carry a mark.

A parent sets the plan once, in the app the family already uses or any certified alternative. Every screen follows it.

Draft for discussion with the Swedish government, 22 October 2026

Thank you for ten minutes. This fits in one picture. A family writes one plan: how long, what, and when, from a first tablet at two to a first phone at fourteen. Every screen in the house follows that plan and reports back. That is Handrail. It is not an app. It is the plan, the common language every device speaks, and a mark that says a product speaks it honestly. A parent sets the plan from whatever app they already use, or from any certified alternative, and switches it off the same way.
