Folkhälsomyndigheten recommendations, Sept 2024 · Proposal sections 3.3 and 4.2

12 / 20

This is the whole plan for one child. A budget, a bedtime, the allowed hours, and a few rules about which apps count how. The starting point for a Swedish family can be the Public Health Agency’s own hours by age, and the parent changes what they like. Every rule has a reason, and the child sees the rule and the reason in the same words the parent does. No secret rules, no hidden monitoring. A parent who is tired on a Tuesday evening can read it in one go, and so can an eleven-year-old, on whichever screen she picks up.
