Backing paper, section 3.1

6 / 14

The minimum age stays. It becomes the default every certified platform applies to any child account with no plan. The child nobody is helping is covered automatically. A parent who does engage carries a plan through the family account they already have: time budgets, windows, bedtime and rules per app. The platform gets the rules, never the child’s record. One limit: this ends age checks only for accounts that arrive with a plan or a parent-set age band. A platform that passes the tests carries the Handrail mark.
