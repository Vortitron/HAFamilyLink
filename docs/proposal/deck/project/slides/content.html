A plan is not only time. Parents decide what: which apps and sites are allowed, which topics and words are blocked, in whatever languages the child uses. Most will copy a community profile and change a few lines. One limit on parents, and it is deliberate. A short list stays open on every certified device whatever the plan says: emergency numbers, the children’s helpline, public health advice, how to report abuse, and children’s rights. A child must always be able to reach help. Which other topics join that list is for the steward to decide, with children’s bodies at the table.
