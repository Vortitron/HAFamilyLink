Everything about a child stays in the home, in certified software on a phone, hub or router. Only the effects of a plan cross the boundary, coarsened, under an identifier unique to each platform. The AI guide runs in the home by default and has no field for content, messages or location. Certification enforces that. Apple has already argued in public against store-wide age verification. Handrail turns that argument into a standard. It also answers the privacy half of the French ruling.
