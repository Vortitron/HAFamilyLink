The floor is the law’s age limit. The inquiry proposes 15, and that stays. How platforms find the child whose parents install nothing is the law’s age-check question, not ours. Handrail adds no check of its own. The path is for parents who want to do more. It does not lower the age. The inquiry chose no parental consent, and whether a plan may ever lift a child above the floor stays the legislator’s choice. The parent’s rules loosen as the child shows they can stop, choose and judge.
