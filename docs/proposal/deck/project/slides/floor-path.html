The floor is the law’s age limit. The inquiry proposes 15, and that stays. On every certified platform it is the default for any child account without a plan, including the child whose parents never install anything. That is the hard case that drives a ban. The path is for parents who want to do more. It does not lower the age. The inquiry chose no parental consent, and whether a plan may ever lift a child above the floor stays the legislator’s choice. The parent’s rules loosen as the child shows they can stop, choose and judge.
