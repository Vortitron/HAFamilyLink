Here is what a tired parent actually wants. Set the rules once. Two hours a day, and it means two hours in total, not two hours on each device. School apps only on a school day. Or homework time where education apps count and games wait. Then it should just hold, on the tablet, the console, the phone and the telly, whoever made them. And the child should see the same rule, in the same words, with the reason. That is the whole vision. Everything else in this deck is how we get there.
