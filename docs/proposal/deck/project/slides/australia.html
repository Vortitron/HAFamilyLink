Australia is where the evidence comes from. It commenced on 10 December 2025 and platforms removed or restricted 4.7 million accounts in the first half of December. Accounts are not children. When eSafety went back to more than 4,000 children and families in March, over 81 per cent of 10 to 15 year olds were still on at least one restricted platform, from about 86 per cent before. Half of those who kept accounts said they were never asked their age. The evidence is early. It already points one way.
