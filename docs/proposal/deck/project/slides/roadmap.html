Your ban is pencilled for spring 2027. No certified platform can exist before 2028. Nothing here asks you to wait or repeal. The steward starts as a free, open standards group at W3C, becomes a European non-profit on the Let’s Encrypt model once two independent implementations conform, and opens membership from 2029, with a permanent child-rights seat, a privacy seat and platform seats capped at half. Dues cover the cost only at roughly 25 members, 2031 at the earliest. Who pays before that is a government, or nobody.
