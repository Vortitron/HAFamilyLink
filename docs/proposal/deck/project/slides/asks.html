1

One clause in secondary legislation: a certified graduated household plan may meet the minimum-age duty, with a safe harbour. It also accepts the parent-declared age band on a family account as a lawful age signal.

2

A named pilot sponsorship of about 3.5 million EUR, the order of Australia’s A$6.5 million age-assurance trial.

3

A twelve-month review point written into the ban’s own commencement.

Three asks fit your calendar. First, one clause with a safe harbour. It also accepts the family account’s parent-declared age band as a lawful age signal. It commits you to nothing until a mark exists and gives platforms a reason to build toward it. Second, a pilot of 400 households across income bands, data held by a university under its own ethics approval. Third, a review date at which the regulator reports the pilot’s outcomes beside the ban’s. Judge both instruments on the same evidence.
