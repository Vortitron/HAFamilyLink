19 / 20

Four asks, all possible from where Sweden stands today, whichever government forms. First, one clause in the coming bill: a parent-managed account carrying a certified household plan meets the platform’s duty, and a platform that applies the plan honestly has met its legal duty. Second, ask the Public Health Agency to publish its hours as the default Swedish plan. Third, sponsor a pilot with 400 households and an independent evaluation, published whatever it finds. Fourth, tell Brussels before 3 December that this is how the parent-controlled tier should work, from any vendor.
