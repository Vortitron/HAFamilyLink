19 / 20

Four asks, all possible from where Sweden stands today, whichever government forms. First, one clause in the coming bill that recognises certified household plans and names who may certify them. It puts no new duty on platforms, because the inquiry found Sweden cannot add duties under the EU’s Digital Services Act. Second, ask the Public Health Agency to publish its hours as the default Swedish plan. Third, sponsor a pilot with 400 households and an independent evaluation, published whatever it finds. Fourth, in the KIDS Act feedback before 3 December and in the Council: the proposal already says the largest platforms’ parental tools must work with other companies’ tools. Ask that a certified open standard counts as meeting that rule, and that platforms which apply it honestly are protected. The Ministry of Culture leads on the file and consults in November.
