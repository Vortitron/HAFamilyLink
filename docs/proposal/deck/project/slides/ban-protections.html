When a child is banned, the easy fix at home is a parent’s account, or a new account with an adult birthday. The moment that happens, every protection built for teenagers disappears. On Instagram a teen account is private, strangers cannot message, there is a sleep mode and a time reminder. On TikTok under-16s cannot be messaged at all. An adult account has none of that. The ban does not remove the child. It removes the protection. Meanwhile the money goes on checking adults and chasing children.
