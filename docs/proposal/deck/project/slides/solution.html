5 / 20

The same five apps, the same child, the same room. Nothing new is installed. In the middle is the plan the parent wrote once: two hours, bedtime, school days. Each vendor’s service receives that plan and applies it on its own screens. Each reports back what it actually did. The parent sets the plan from whichever of the five they already use, because once certified any of them can carry the whole household. A family that prefers an independent app, a website or a home hub can use that instead. Matter did this for the smart home: Apple, Google and Amazon kept their own apps and agreed one language.
