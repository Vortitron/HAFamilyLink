Consultation responses ask what goes with the age limit

Justitiekanslern (Chancellor of Justice), 15 Oct

A significant intrusion into children’s rights, proposed without weighing alternatives.

“utan … alternativa lösningar”

Bris, 16 Oct

A ban alone will not keep children safe. The main duty now lies with the platforms.

“Det primära ansvaret … ligger nu på plattformarna”

Barnombudsmannen (Children’s Ombudsman), 18 Sept

A limit can never answer all risks. Pair the law with support to parents.

“kan aldrig vara ett svar på alla risker”

The children, in SOU 2026:35

A limit is easy to get round. We will move to less regulated places.

“lätt att kringgå”

Internetstiftelsen rejects the proposal as drafted. Mediemyndigheten accepts a limit but says it cannot handle the risks alone.

Remissvar on SOU 2026:35, September to October 2026, published on regeringen.se · Dagens Juridik · Bris via TT

10 / 20

The consultation closed last Friday, 16 October. Several key responses ask the same question. The Chancellor of Justice says the inquiry proposed a limit without setting out alternatives. Bris says a ban alone is not enough and the platforms must do the work. The Children’s Ombudsman wants the law paired with support to parents. And the children told the inquiry they would simply move somewhere less regulated. None of these bodies is against protecting children. They are asking what else goes with the floor. The rest of this deck is an answer to that question.
