Handrail, draft for discussion, October 2026 · Proposal sections 4.3 and 4.7

14 / 20

Trust comes from one rule. Today a parent presses a button and hopes. Under the mark, every certified device or service must look at its own state and report back: done, partly done, could not, or do not know. If it does not know, it says so and the parent sees amber. It is never allowed to show locked when it is not sure. Grey means this device is not in the standard yet, so the household uses the router instead. A vendor that fails silently loses the mark, after notice and a chance to fix it.
