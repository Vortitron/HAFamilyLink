Three countries have been here first. Australia banned under-16s in December 2025. Three months later more than 81 per cent of 10 to 15 year olds were still using a restricted platform, down from about 86. The UK ran 69 million age checks in six months, and one vendor handling appeals leaked about 70,000 ID photos. France’s constitutional court struck down its under-15 law in August, partly because it forced every adult to prove their age and gave parents no say. The evidence is early. It already points one way.
