# Annexes to the Handrail paper

Supplementary papers to `../PROPOSAL.md`, researched and fact-checked on 22 October 2026. Each one was written by one researcher and then checked by a second, who opened every cited source. Claims that could not be confirmed are marked in the text.

| Annex | What it adds |
| --- | --- |
| [What outside software can do with vendors' parental controls today](annex-vendor-parental-control-apis.md) | Vendor by vendor, what another app can read, set and read back today, and which parts of Handrail could run now. |
| [The legal basis and legal routes in Sweden and the EU](annex-legal-routes-sweden-eu.md) | The DSA, the 2025 guidelines on minors, the KIDS Act, GDPR, portability law, the Convention on the Rights of the Child, the AI Act, and where a safe harbour can sit. |
| [What the research says works](annex-what-the-research-says-works.md) | Parental guidance, controls, monitoring and sleep, with Swedish and Nordic evidence, and what the pilot should measure. |
| [How other countries handle parental controls](annex-international-parental-controls-lessons.md) | France, the UK, Australia, four US states, China, South Korea, Norway, Denmark and Ireland, with lessons for Sweden. |
| [Who to approach, and how to pay for it](annex-approach-and-funding.md) | Swedish, Nordic and EU bodies, funding programmes, and an order of approach for November 2026 to January 2027. |
| [Checking the paper's figures against primary sources](annex-figure-check-primary-sources.md) | Every number and dated fact in the paper, checked against its original source. Written for the author. |
